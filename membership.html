How membership works

Our club is open to all, with varying levels of participation. Every role is open to any UWM student.

1

General Member

Come to meetings, join the discussion, and vote on portfolio decisions. You're a member the day you show up.

2

Sector Analyst

Cover a sector with a small team or by yourself. Follow the news on our sector holdings and report to the club once a month. You'll be guided by senior club members, and it's a great addition to any resume.

Sector Analyst sign-up form Sector Analyst examples
3

Board

Positions are elected each spring. Different roles aim to find a fit for everyone.
